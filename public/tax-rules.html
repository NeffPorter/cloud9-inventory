🧾 Store Tax Rules

Set per-store excise or category tax rates. These show as "Effective Cost" on distributor price sheets.

How it works: Tax rules apply to a specific category at a specific location. For example, Nevada stores may have a ~30% excise tax on vapeable products. When viewing a distributor's price list for that store, the effective cost (price + tax) is shown alongside the base price.
